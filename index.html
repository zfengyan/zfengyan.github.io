Fengyan Zhang

Geospatial & Data

Previously a research assistant at RWTH Aachen, prior to which I completed my Master’s in Geomatics at TU Delft. My work moves between algorithms and geometry—developing clean, efficient tools for processing complex 2D/3D forms, mostly through C++ and Python. For my thesis, I built snapoly, a prototype for snap-rounding polygons through constrained Delaunay triangulation. I have also worked with IoT-driven sensor networks, handling real-time data collection, storage, and visualization. My current interests span spatial data processing, 3D reconstruction, and the evolving landscape of urban innovation. I work at the intersection of geospatial technology, sensor data, and quiet narratives about cities.

This space, as I hope, is a calm outpost — part portfolio, part notebook, part observatory. For the things I am building, reading, and trying to understand.
